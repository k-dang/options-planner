# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Options learners and experienced self-directed options traders. Learners use the product to understand strategy mechanics and trade outcomes; experienced traders use it to evaluate a specific trade and compare candidate strategies efficiently.

## Product Purpose

Options Planner helps people learn, model, compare, and monitor US equity options strategies before they take action. It combines understandable visuals with credible analytics so users can explore ideas, inspect a trade's risk and payoff, and narrow down candidates with confidence.

## Positioning

An options-planning tool that makes sophisticated strategy analysis visually clear, quick to compare, and credible enough for serious self-directed traders.

## Brand Personality

Modern, snappy, and confident. The product should make complex financial decisions feel legible and deliberate, without making light of the risk involved.

## Anti-references

Avoid the generic SaaS-dashboard look: decorative metric tiles, interchangeable card grids, weak information hierarchy, and visual polish that does not help a trader make a decision.

## Design Principles

- Make the shape of risk and reward immediately legible.
- Let visual clarity accelerate comparison without hiding analytical depth.
- Support learning and expert workflows in the same composed interface.
- Treat models and estimates as decision support, not certainty.
- Keep the interface fast, focused, and confident under dense information.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No additional requirements were specified. Preserve readable contrast, clear state communication beyond color alone, keyboard-operable controls, and reduced-motion support as baseline product quality.
